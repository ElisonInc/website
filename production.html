Quoted to the project. Not a template.

A product demo, a 15-second ad and a multi-video campaign have different requirements. We quote production separately based on scope, runtime, aspect ratios, deliverables, revisions, licensing and turnaround.

AI-assisted methods may be part of the workflow. The proposed approach, client asset permissions, intellectual-property and usage terms are addressed before production begins. A discovery conversation is not an agreement to produce a video. Review client, ownership and payment information.

Before we begin

Production questions.

Do you only make AI-generated videos?

No single method fits every brief. Eclipse Studio is our internal AI-assisted production workflow, and we determine the creative approach, source assets and any additional production needs when we scope the project.

Can you make different versions for social and ads?

Yes, when included in the quote. Specify where the videos will run, required aspect ratios, runtime and number of variations so the deliverables are clear.

Who owns and can use the finished video?

Usage, ownership, licensing, third-party assets and permissions depend on the project. These terms should be agreed in writing before production starts rather than assumed from a preview. Review project information.
