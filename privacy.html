A plain-language explanation of how inquiries work on this website.

Updated November 9, 2026.

What we collect

When you submit a consulting inquiry, you may provide your name, email address, company or project, requested service, approximate budget and message. Please do not include passwords, financial account details, sensitive customer information or other confidential material in the initial form.

How we use it

We use inquiry details to read and respond to your request, assess whether a service is a fit, and communicate about possible engagements. Sending an inquiry does not create a client relationship or book an appointment.

Services involved

The inquiry form uses FormSubmit to forward submitted information to info@elisoninc.com. Scheduling links lead to Calendly; payment links lead to Stripe. Those providers handle data under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not collect payment card numbers directly through this website.

Website operation

Hosting, browser requests and third-party links may involve technical information such as IP addresses and device or browser data. External websites linked here operate independently. We do not control their tracking or privacy practices.

Retention and requests

Communications may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to follow up on inquiries, manage business arrangements or comply with applicable obligations. To ask about an inquiry, request a correction or request deletion of information we hold, email info@elisoninc.com. Some records may need to be retained for legal or business reasons.

Changes

We may update this page as the website or service providers change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

Contact

Questions?

For privacy questions, correction or deletion requests, reach us through our official contact email.
