Privacy &
contact data.

A plain-language overview of the information involved in inquiries, scheduling and payments.

Updated November 9, 2026.

Information you choose to provide

Our inquiry form asks for your name, email address, and a description of the project. You may also choose to share a company name, service type and approximate budget. Emails and scheduling or payment requests can involve further details you provide directly to those providers. Please do not submit passwords, customer records, full payment card details, confidential source files, medical information or other sensitive material through the public inquiry form.

How information is used

We use inquiry details to review and respond to requests, determine whether a project is a fit, communicate about estimates or appointments, keep business correspondence, and administer any subsequent relationship. Submitting a form does not create a client relationship, book a meeting, or enroll you in a subscription or newsletter. We do not ask you to send payment card numbers by email or through this form.

Vendors and sharing

Our public website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Our inquiry form uses FormSubmit, which forwards the submitted details to info@elisoninc.com. Appointment requests open Calendly; one-time consultation payments open Stripe, which processes the checkout. Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Each external service processes technical or submitted information under its own policies. We do not directly collect card details or store them on this website. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to provide requested services, protect rights or comply with legal obligations.

Cookies and technical information

Browsers, site hosting and linked providers may process information such as IP address, browser type, device data, timestamps and referring pages. External booking and checkout pages may set cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. We do not represent that third-party links are free from tracking. We will update this notice if analytics, advertising tags or other tracking tools are deployed directly on the website.

Retention, access and deletion requests

We retain inquiries and project communications only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes above and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Retention periods can differ depending on whether an inquiry becomes a paid project. You may email info@elisoninc.com to request access, correction or deletion of information we control. We may ask you to verify the request and may need to retain records required by law or necessary to resolve legitimate business matters. For information controlled directly by Calendly, Stripe or another provider, their own privacy processes may also apply.

Security and confidential submissions

We aim to limit the information collected and access to it. No website or email service can promise absolute security. Do not send secrets or regulated personal information through our public contact channels. If a project requires sensitive files, ask us to agree on confidentiality and an appropriate secure transfer method before sharing them.

Children

These business services are not intended for children to submit personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal data through an inquiry, please contact us so we can review it.

Changes

We may update this page as the website or service providers change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

Contact

Questions?

For privacy questions, correction, deletion or accessibility requests, reach us through our official contact email.
