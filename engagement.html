Working
together.

Clear expectations protect the client and the people doing the work. This page explains how an inquiry becomes a project—and what should be agreed before money or sensitive files change hands.

Updated November 9, 2026.

First contact is not a contract

Submitting a form, sending an email or booking a free discovery call does not create a paid engagement. Discovery calls are limited to 15 minutes, are subject to actual availability, and do not include a detailed implementation plan or free production work.

What is agreed before a project starts

For development, consulting beyond the published one-hour session, and creative production, we confirm the client, the objectives, deliverables, milestones, dates, fees, payment schedule, permitted uses, revisions and approval process in a written proposal or services agreement accepted by both sides. Work outside that scope requires a documented change and any resulting price or timeline adjustment. We do not promise results, rankings, sales, audience growth or third-party platform approvals.

Published consultation pricing

The strategy consultation currently costs $200 USD for a 60-minute session focused on one defined challenge with a short written recap. It is a one-time service, not a subscription. Confirm fit, scope and a workable meeting arrangement before paying. Checkout is processed by Stripe; any applicable tax is calculated at checkout. Payment does not automatically book a meeting.

If you paid but have not scheduled or received the service and need to cancel, email info@elisoninc.com promptly with the payment email and request. If we cannot agree on a workable time to provide the unused consultation, we will arrange a refund. Cancellation, rescheduling and refund conditions for other projects are agreed in writing before payment and remain subject to applicable law. Any mandatory consumer protections still apply.

Approvals, revisions and delivery

Each proposal should state who approves work, how many rounds of revisions are included, the delivery format, deadlines and the consequences of delayed feedback or additional requests. Source files, project files, ongoing hosting, editable models, software repositories, raw footage and continuing maintenance are not automatically part of a final deliverable; they must be identified in writing when included.

Ownership, licensing and portfolio use

Before paid work begins, the parties should agree on ownership or licensing of final deliverables and underlying source materials, whether payment is a condition of transfer, permitted channels and territories, use of third-party materials, and any exclusivity. Elison Inc.'s pre-existing tools, methods and internal technology, including Eclipse Studio, do not transfer with a commissioned video or website unless an agreement expressly says otherwise. The client should also know whether any project may be displayed in a portfolio; confidential or unreleased client work is not automatically publicized.

AI-assisted production and client materials

Eclipse Studio may use AI-assisted workflows. We discuss the proposed creative method, material limitations, approvals, disclosure requirements and any third-party licensing or tool restrictions as part of scoping. Clients must have permission to provide any brand assets, music, footage, names, faces, voices or likenesses they supply. We will not knowingly use someone's identity, voice or protected work in a deceptive or unauthorized manner. AI outputs and their intellectual-property treatment can vary; ownership and commercial usage rights should be reviewed for the actual deliverables.

Privacy and confidential information

Do not put passwords, API keys, customer records, trade secrets, medical information, payment details or unpublished proprietary materials into the public inquiry form. Arrange an appropriate confidentiality agreement and secure delivery method before exchanging sensitive documents. See our privacy information for the tools involved in initial contact.

Website and external services

Descriptions, prices and availability on this website may change and do not override an executed written agreement. External services such as Stripe, Calendly, FormSubmit and linked project sites have their own terms. No guarantee is made that a third-party provider, platform, ad account or distribution channel will approve or continue a feature, listing or campaign.

Questions, corrections or disputes

For questions about scope, billing, cancellations, ownership, accessibility or a problem with work delivered, contact info@elisoninc.com. Please include the relevant proposal or transaction reference, without sharing full payment card details. We'll review the issue and respond through the appropriate business channel. Nothing on this page is intended to remove rights or remedies available under applicable law.

This page is informational, not a substitute for a separately agreed, legally reviewed contract. In the event of a conflict, an executed project agreement and applicable law govern the paid engagement.
