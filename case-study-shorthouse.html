Project study / ShortHouse / Pre-launch

Stories built for the way people watch.

ShortHouse is a vertical micro-drama streaming venture developed by Elison Inc. B2, a serialized sci-fi thriller, is in production as its flagship original. The platform has not commercially launched.

The project

A platform and production system.

The opportunity

Short, serial storytelling creates a different viewing rhythm from conventional television: compact episodes, phone-first framing and endings that motivate the next watch.

Product approach

ShortHouse is being built as a streaming destination for original vertical drama. The initial focus is B2, with additional creator distribution considered as a future expansion rather than a current public offering.

Production workflow

Elison Inc. uses internal AI-assisted production tooling, including Eclipse Studio, to support episodic work. Production and review include versioned candidates and independent quality-control feedback rather than treating rough cuts as finished releases.

Current status

The platform is precommercial. B2 production is ongoing and not represented here as a completed, mastered or published series. This project study makes no claims about subscriber counts, revenue or released episode totals.

What this demonstrates

Product, intellectual property and production.

ShortHouse brings together streaming product design, episodic creative development and repeatable production operations—work that requires both editorial decisions and technical systems.
