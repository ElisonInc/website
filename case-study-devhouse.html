DevHouse AI is an Elison Inc. venture focused on practical websites and AI front-office workflows for home-service businesses. This is a company-built product study, not a client testimonial.

The project

Connect the first customer touchpoint.

The problem

Service contractors often work away from a desk. Customer inquiries, scheduling and follow-up still need attention while teams are on jobs. A website alone does not manage that operational gap.

The approach

DevHouse AI combines customer-facing web experiences with an AI receptionist concept called Avery, designed around answering inquiries, collecting job details, qualifying requests and supporting bookings.

Product thinking

The core workflow is straightforward: capture a request, gather enough context, direct it to the right next step and make follow-up easier. Any production deployment needs clear escalation, scheduling rules and integration testing.

Current status

DevHouse AI is open for client conversations. This page describes the product direction and its workflow—not verified customer results, conversion improvements or a claim that all integrations are live for every business.

What this demonstrates

Technology tied to operations.

The work sits at the intersection of customer experience, service operations, AI interaction design and software integration. A useful implementation starts with the business process rather than the model.
