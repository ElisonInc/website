Before we start

Good questions.

What happens on the free discovery call?

We spend up to 15 minutes understanding the goal, budget and timeline, then decide whether there is a fit. Detailed recommendations, architecture and implementation plans belong to a paid engagement.

Do I need a technical background?

No. Bring the problem, who it affects and what a useful outcome would look like. We can translate that into product requirements and technical options.

Does a consultation include development?

No. The consultation covers advice and a written recap. Roadmaps and implementation have their own agreed scope and fees.

How do payment and scheduling work?

Book a free discovery call through Calendly to confirm fit and scope, or contact us by email. The strategy consultation can then be paid through the secure Stripe checkout linked above. Applicable tax is calculated at checkout. Meeting times are arranged by email; payment does not automatically reserve an appointment.

Can we work remotely?

Yes. Consulting can take place remotely. Any in-person work, ongoing support or additional meetings must be included in the proposal.

Can I share a confidential idea?

Keep the initial inquiry high level. Do not send passwords, customer records or confidential documents. If an NDA or specific IP terms are needed, agree them before sharing sensitive material or starting work.
