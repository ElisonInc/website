# Elison Inc. — Client Engagement Checklist
**Internal operating checklist — not an executed contract or legal advice. Have qualified counsel review the agreement before relying on it.**

## Before accepting money or sensitive material
- [ ] Identify the correct legal contracting parties, legal entity name, authorized signers, contact and billing information.
- [ ] Determine business/consumer classification and any jurisdiction-specific disclosure, taxes, refund or cancellation rules.
- [ ] Agree the client objective, audience, intended platforms, delivery deadline and acceptance contact.
- [ ] Put a signed statement of work (SOW) or engagement letter in place for paid project work.
- [ ] Use a separate NDA and appropriate secure file-transfer system **before** asking for trade secrets, passwords or customer information.
- [ ] Confirm what third-party tools, platforms and accounts are involved, and who owns/accesses each account.
- [ ] Do not promise a marketing result, search ranking, approval, specific revenue, AI feature, or rights that have not been established.

## Written scope and financial controls
- [ ] Precisely list deliverables, formats, sizes, aspect ratios, languages, runtime, number of versions and required functionality.
- [ ] List what is **not** included: source files, raw footage, project files, code repository, hosting, maintenance, future software updates, stock/music licenses or ad spend unless separately specified.
- [ ] Identify client-provided input materials, approvals, credentials and deadlines; note dependencies and schedule impacts.
- [ ] Document creative approvals, review windows, number of included revisions and when additional changes become paid change orders.
- [ ] State fees, billing milestones, taxes, payment method, payment timing, expenses and which work starts after payment.
- [ ] Explain fair rescheduling, cancellation, termination, refunds for undelivered work and what happens if either party cannot complete the project, subject to applicable law.
- [ ] Agree a delivery and acceptance process, correction period and how disputes/defects are documented.
- [ ] Define support, warranty/bug-fix period (if any) and who handles hosting/account expenses after handoff.

## Rights and client protection
- [ ] Verify the client's ownership or permission to use their brand, footage, photos, fonts, music, voices, trademarks, likenesses and supplied references.
- [ ] Specify who owns or licenses **final paid deliverables**, when rights transfer, whether exclusivity applies, and any geographic/media/term restrictions.
- [ ] Reserve **pre-existing Elison Inc. technology, production tooling, prompts/workflows, code libraries, internal processes and Eclipse Studio IP** unless specifically licensed in writing.
- [ ] List all third-party assets, open-source components, AI providers and stock-media licenses; confirm the actual commercial usage and redistribution permissions.
- [ ] Define whether either party may use finished work in a public portfolio or testimonial. Obtain client approval for confidential/unreleased projects and accurate attribution.
- [ ] If generative AI is used, explain the workflow and limitations, review outputs for identifiable people, lookalikes, synthetic voices, misleading claims, infringing content and required disclosures.
- [ ] Allocate responsibility for legal/regulatory review of ad copy, claims, endorsements and final publication (especially for healthcare, finance, minors or regulated businesses).
- [ ] Require client approval of final claims/creative, and an escalation path for any misleading/unsubstantiated statement.

## Data security and operational controls
- [ ] Do not receive sensitive records through FormSubmit/public email. Set up a secure transfer method and limit access.
- [ ] Agree any data-processing terms and roles if work involves customer PII, recordings, confidential datasets or external integrations.
- [ ] Implement least-privilege access, MFA on business accounts, key/credential storage outside repositories, documented offboarding and security incident reporting.
- [ ] Remove access and delete/return client data as defined by the signed agreement, operational requirements and applicable law.
- [ ] Keep written records of client approval, scope change requests, invoices, receipts and deliverable handoff.
- [ ] Review business insurance, contractual liability limits, indemnities and dispute venue with counsel. Do **not** assume a website page creates enforceable protections.

## One-time $200 strategy consultation
- [ ] Confirm service fit and a workable time before sending the Stripe payment link.
- [ ] Confirm exactly 60 minutes, one defined challenge and short written recap.
- [ ] Explain that the payment is one-time, taxes where applicable, and no Calendly time is reserved by paying.
- [ ] If the session is unused and no workable time can be arranged, arrange a refund promptly.
- [ ] Confirm completion, recap delivery and obtain feedback before closing the engagement.

## Publication gate
A project is not ready for release until **client approves the content, deliverables and permitted usage**, files are tested, licenses are checked, applicable disclosure requirements are satisfied, and any promised source files or documentation are delivered.

**Important:** Elison Inc. should have an attorney adapt a master services agreement and SOW to its actual legal entity, states of operation and client mix. This checklist is a workflow tool, not legal protection on its own.
